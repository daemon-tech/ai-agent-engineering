## Observations worth keeping

1. **Verification is the recurring theme.** From SWE-bench's fail-to-pass tests [@jimenez2024swebench] **[PR]** to this week's oracle audits [@chen2026oracle] **[PP]** and bug-discovery benchmarks [@yao2026sweeper] **[PP]**, the hard problem is knowing whether an output is right, not only producing one. This motivated making the verification/evaluation distinction central to the taxonomy **[OP]**.
2. **The term "Agentic Software Engineering" is already in use** [@hassan2025agenticse] **[PP]**, [@hassan2026se3] **[PR]**. We adopt it and do not claim it. "Harness engineering" is also emerging [@zhou2026externalization] **[PP]**. AAE's distinctive contribution should be methodological (evidence-labelled synthesis), not terminological.
3. **ReAct is often over-cited.** The paper shows that ReAct alone trailed CoT on HotpotQA and that hybrids performed best [@yao2023react] **[PR]**, which is weaker than the "ReAct beats reasoning-only" reading common in practitioner writing.
4. **pstack documents practices, not outcomes.** Its verification design is detailed and in places close to what recent preprints test (e.g., measurement-gated instruction files [@giampieri2026cortex] **[PP]**), but the repository reports no measured effects [@tan2026pstack] **[EE]**.
5. **Tooling friction during verification.** OpenReview and dblp blocked automated access today. ICLR acceptances were confirmed through ICLR's own proceedings and virtual-conference pages instead. AutoGen's and τ-bench's venues could not be confirmed, so both stay preprints for now.
